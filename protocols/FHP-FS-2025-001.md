# 🌟 The Fairness Score (FHP FS v1.0)

The Fairness Score is a bidirectional accountability metric:
- 	For companies: it measures how fairly they behave
- 	For candidates: it measures how fairly the system treats them

It’s not a vibe. It’s not a reputation score. It’s a mathematically defined, auditable, versioned metric.

It protects candidates from discriminatory companies, and it protects companies from unfair accusations by grounding everything in data, transparency, and protocol defined rules.

And it’s built from the traces, match logs, and governance rules we’ve already defined.

*Let’s break it down.*

## 1. Company Fairness Score (C FS)

This is the score that determines:
- 	whether a company is compliant
- 	whether they get warnings
- 	whether their jobs get suspended
- 	whether they get publicly flagged
- 	whether they can continue using the protocol

It’s composed of five weighted components.

### C FS Component 1 — Response Compliance (30%)

This is where ghosting detection feeds in.

Metrics include:
- 	% of matches responded to within SLA
- 	% of structured rejections provided
- 	% of ghosted candidates
- 	average response time

Formula (simplified):
`response_score = 1 - ghosting_rate`

*This alone catches a huge amount of unfair behaviour.*

### C FS Component 2 — Rejection Validity (25%)

This measures whether companies use the structured rejection taxonomy correctly.

Metrics include:
- 	% of valid rejection codes
- 	% of rejections contradicted by trace
- 	% of forbidden reasons attempted
- 	% of appeals upheld against the company

Formula (simplified):
`rejection_score = valid_rejections / total_rejections`

*This prevents “culture fit” and other discriminatory nonsense.*

### C FS Component 3 — Bias Metrics (30%)

This is the statistical fairness engine.

Metrics include:
- 	disparate impact
- 	equal opportunity difference
- 	score skew
- 	demographic consistency
- 	bias correction frequency

Formula (simplified):
`bias_score = 1 - normalized_bias_metric`

*If a company consistently disadvantages a protected group, this score drops.*

### C FS Component 4 — Job Brief Quality (10%)

This measures whether companies write fair, clear, compliant job briefs.

Metrics include:
- 	must have clarity
- 	salary range provided
- 	no illegal filters
- 	no contradictory constraints
- 	no “years of experience” as a proxy for age

Formula (simplified):
`job_quality_score = compliant_job_briefs / total_job_briefs`

*This prevents companies from gaming the system.*

### C FS Component 5 — Governance Compliance (5%)

This measures whether companies:
- 	respond to governance requests
- 	complete audits
- 	fix violations
- 	acknowledge warnings

Formula (simplified):
`governance_score = completed_actions / required_actions`

## Final Company Fairness Score
```
C-FS = 
0.30 * response_score +
0.25 * rejection_score +
0.30 * bias_score +
0.10 * job_quality_score +
0.05 * governance_score
```

Scores are from 0.00 to 1.00.

Thresholds:
- 	0.80–1.00 → Fully compliant
- 	0.65–0.79 → Warning
- 	0.50–0.64 → Job suspension
- 	< 0.50 → Company suspension + public fairness report

*This is how we enforce fairness at scale.*

## 2. Candidate Fairness Score (P FS)

This is not a “rating” of the candidate. It’s a measure of how fairly the system treated them.

It answers:
- 	Did the system infer their skills correctly?
- 	Did the system misinterpret their experience?
- 	Did bias correction apply?
- 	Did they receive timely responses?
- 	Did they receive structured rejections?
- 	Did they need to appeal?

*This protects candidates.*

### P FS Component 1 — Interpretation Accuracy (40%)

Based on:
- 	candidate corrections
- 	inference disagreements
- 	appeals upheld
- 	ontology mismatches

Formula (simplified):

`interpretation_score = 1 - correction_rate`

### P FS Component 2 — Response Fairness (30%)

Based on:
- 	ghosting events
- 	SLA compliance
- 	structured rejection quality
Formula:
`response_fairness = 1 - ghosting_events_for_candidate`

### P FS Component 3 — Bias Correction (30%)

Based on:
- 	whether bias correction was applied
- 	whether it should have been
- 	whether appeals triggered correction

Formula:
`bias_fairness = 1 - missed_bias_corrections`

### Final Candidate Fairness Score

```
P-FS = 
0.40 * interpretation_score +
0.30 * response_fairness +
0.30 * bias_fairness
```

*This gives candidates a transparent view of how the system treated them.*

## 3. Why Fairness Scores Matter

**For candidates**
- 	They can see which companies behave fairly
- 	They can avoid bad actors
- 	They can appeal unfair treatment
- 	They can trust the system

**For companies**
- 	They get a clear, objective fairness benchmark
- 	They can improve behaviour
- 	They can demonstrate compliance
- 	They can avoid legal risk

**For governance**
- 	They can detect systemic issues
- 	They can enforce rules
- 	They can publish fairness reports
- 	They can suspend bad actors

**For the ecosystem**
- 	It creates a race to the top
- 	It eliminates silent discrimination
- 	It makes fairness measurable
- 	It makes fairness enforceable

*This is the first hiring system where fairness is not a slogan — it’s a score.*
