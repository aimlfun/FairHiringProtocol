# Minimum Fairness Standard (MFS) v0.1 — Draft

## 1. Transparency as a First Class Principle

## 1.1. Explainability

Every match must include:
- why the candidate was recommended
- which criteria mattered
- which criteria were ignored
- how the score was computed

## 1.2. Rejection Transparency

Every rejection must include:
- the specific criteria that caused the mismatch
- whether those criteria were “hard” or “soft”
- whether the candidate could address the gap

## 1.3. Public Matching Logic

The matching algorithm must be:
- open-source
- versioned
- auditable
- reproducible

No black boxes.

---

## 2. Candidate Autonomy & Data Ownership

## 2.1. Candidate Controlled Profiles

Candidates must be able to:
- choose what data to include
- hide irrelevant or bias triggering attributes
- correct AI inferred skills
- delete their profile at any time

## 2.2. No Data Lock In

All candidate data must be:
- exportable
- portable
- interoperable

## 2.3. Consent Driven Matching

Candidates choose:
- which roles they want to be matched to
- which companies can see their profile
- what visibility level they prefer

---

## 3. Structured, Semantic, Bias Aware Matching

## 3.1. Structured Inputs

Both job briefs and candidate profiles must be converted into:
- structured skills
- structured experience
- structured preferences
- structured constraints

## 3.2. Semantic Equivalence
The system must understand:
- synonyms
- related skills
- transferable skills
- seniority equivalence

## 3.3. Bias Aware Scoring
The system must:
- detect bias
- measure bias
- counteract bias
- report bias

Bias blind ≠ fair. Bias aware = fair.

---

## 4. Community Governance & Open Protocols

## 4.1. Open Protocol

The matching system must be:
- protocol based
- client agnostic
- model agnostic
- open to extensions

## 4.2. Community Review

Changes to the matching logic must:
- be proposed publicly
- be reviewed publicly
- include fairness impact assessments

## 4.3. Forkability

Anyone must be able to:
- fork the matching logic
- propose alternative scoring modules
- build their own UI or agent

This prevents centralization of power.

---

## 5. Fairness Metrics & Continuous Monitoring

## 5.1. Protected Attribute Monitoring

The system must track:
- age
- gender
- ethnicity
- disability
- socioeconomic background
- career gaps
- education level

Not to use them in scoring — but to detect bias in scoring.

## 5.2. Outcome Parity Metrics

The system must measure:
- recommendation parity
- interview parity
- offer parity
- salary parity

## 5.3. Drift Detection

If the system begins to:
- favor certain demographics
- favor certain universities
- favor certain job titles
- penalize older candidates

…it must flag this automatically.

---

## 6. Human Centered Design

## 6.1. No Ghosting

The system must ensure:
- candidates always receive feedback
- companies cannot silently ignore applicants

## 6.2. No Arbitrary Filters

Companies cannot filter by:
- age
- years since graduation
- university prestige
- job title keywords

## 6.3. Candidate Dignity

The system must:
- avoid ranking humans as “better”
- focus on fit, not worth
- avoid language that dehumanizes

---

## 7. Ethical Constraints on Companies

## 7.1. Honest Job Descriptions

Companies must:
- provide real requirements
- distinguish “must have” from “nice to have”
- disclose salary ranges

## 7.2. No Fishing Expeditions
Companies cannot:
- post fake jobs
- collect CVs without intent to hire
- use the system for market research

## 7.3. Accountability
Companies must:
- justify rejections
- follow fair process
- adhere to the fairness standard

---

## 8. Model Independence & Multi Model Validation

## 8.1. No Single Model Dependence

Matching must be validated by:
- multiple models
- multiple scoring modules
- multiple perspectives

## 8.2. Consensus Scoring

A match is only valid if:
- multiple models agree
- bias checks pass
- explanations align

This prevents model specific quirks from becoming systemic bias.

---

## 9. Safety, Privacy, and Security

## 9.1. Minimal Data Retention

Store only what is necessary.

## 9.2. Zero Knowledge Matching (Long Term Goal)

Eventually:
- companies never see raw candidate data
- they only see match explanations
- candidates reveal themselves only when they choose

## 9.3. No Surveillance

No scraping, no tracking, no behavioral profiling.

---

## 10. Fairness as a Living Standard

This standard must:
- evolve
- be community driven
- be evidence based
- be versioned
- be forkable

*Fairness is not a static state. It’s a continuous process.*
