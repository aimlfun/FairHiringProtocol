# Fair Hiring Protocol — Code of Conduct (v1.0)

## Purpose

The Fair Hiring Protocol (FHP) exists to promote fairness, transparency, accountability, and dignity in hiring. This Code of Conduct defines the standards of behaviour expected from everyone participating in the project — contributors, maintainers, companies, researchers, and community members.

Our goal is to create a safe, respectful, and collaborative environment where people can work together to build a fairer hiring ecosystem.

## Our Values

We commit to:
- Fairness — treating all participants with respect and without discrimination
- Transparency — being open about decisions, processes, and governance
- Accountability — owning our actions and correcting mistakes
- Inclusivity — welcoming diverse perspectives and backgrounds
- Constructive collaboration — solving problems together
- Human dignity — recognising the real‑world impact of hiring systems

These values guide both the protocol and the community around it.

## Expected Behaviour
All participants are expected to:

## Be respectful
- Treat others with kindness and professionalism
- Assume good intent, even when disagreeing
- Critique ideas, not people

## Be inclusive
- Welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds
- Avoid exclusionary language or behaviour
- Make space for underrepresented voices

## Be constructive
- Provide helpful, actionable feedback
- Focus on solutions, not blame
- Collaborate rather than compete

## Be transparent
- Disclose conflicts of interest
- Document decisions clearly
- Follow the governance process

## Be responsible
- Protect sensitive information
- Avoid misrepresenting the protocol
- Use the protocol ethically and in good faith

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not tolerated:
- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language
- Personal attacks or insults
- Dismissing or belittling contributors
- Sharing private or confidential information
- Using the protocol to enable discriminatory hiring practices
- Misrepresenting the protocol for commercial or political gain
- Attempting to bypass fairness, transparency, or accountability mechanisms
- Any behaviour that undermines the safety or dignity of participants

## Responsibilities of Maintainers

## Project maintainers are responsible for:
- Upholding this Code of Conduct
- Reviewing contributions fairly and respectfully
- Enforcing governance rules consistently
- Communicating decisions clearly
- Taking corrective action when necessary

### Maintainers have the right to:
- Edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this Code
- Issue warnings or temporary bans
- Permanently remove participants who engage in harmful behaviour

### Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct:
- Open a confidential issue via the repository’s reporting mechanism
- Or contact the maintainers directly (contact details in the repo)

Reports will be handled promptly, respectfully, and confidentially.

## Enforcement
Violations of this Code may result in:
- A private warning
- Temporary suspension from participation
- Removal from the project
- Public documentation of governance actions (for severe cases)

The severity of the response will match the severity of the behaviour.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces (GitHub, discussions, issues, PRs)
- Community events
- External communications related to the project
- Any use of the protocol that affects the community or its reputation

## Commitment to Improvement

The Fair Hiring Protocol is a living project.

We commit to revisiting and improving this Code of Conduct as the community grows and as we learn from experience.
