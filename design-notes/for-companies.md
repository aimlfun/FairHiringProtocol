# Fair Hiring Protocol — Guide for Companies

The Fair Hiring Protocol (FHP) helps companies hire fairly, transparently, and effectively.

This guide explains how to participate responsibly.

---

# 1. What FHP Provides

- High quality matches  
- Transparent explanations  
- Bias aware scoring  
- Reduced legal risk  
- Improved candidate trust  
- Clear job brief structure  

---

# 2. Your Responsibilities

Companies must:

- Provide salary ranges  
- Provide real responsibilities  
- Separate must have from nice to have  
- Avoid arbitrary filters  
- Respond to matched candidates  
- Provide structured rejection reasons  
- Participate in fairness audits  

See `company-compliance.md` for full requirements.

---

# 3. How Matching Works

The system evaluates:

- skills  
- experience  
- transferable skills  
- preferences  
- constraints  
- fairness metrics  

You receive a structured explanation for every match.

---

# 4. Benefits of Compliance

- Better candidate quality  
- Reduced bias  
- Improved hiring outcomes  
- Stronger employer brand  
- Lower legal exposure  

---

# 5. Violations

Violations may result in:

- warnings  
- job suspension  
- company suspension  
- public fairness reports  

---

**FHP helps you hire the people you need — fairly, transparently, and responsibly.**
