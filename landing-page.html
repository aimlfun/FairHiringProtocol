The matching pipeline

Nine stages.
Every one auditable.

01
Normalise
Skill IDs are validated against the FHP ontology. Salaries converted to a common basis. Location data resolved.
02
Expand
Skills are expanded with synonyms and related terms. Python matches Python 3. React matches ReactJS. No penalisation for naming conventions.
03
Constrain
Hard requirements are checked. A missing must-have skill stops the pipeline here with a specific, plain-language explanation.
04
Score skills
Must-have and nice-to-have skills are scored against declared proficiency using a public mathematical formula.
05
Transfer
72 ontology-defined transfer relationships recognise related skills. Docker experience counts towards Kubernetes. Spark counts towards data engineering.
06
Align preferences
Salary, work mode, and location compatibility contribute to the composite score. Hard mismatches are handled at Stage 3.
07
Detect bias
Three fairness metrics — DIR, EOD, SDS — are checked for the candidate's demographic cohort. Data-insufficient cohorts are flagged, not guessed at.
08
Correct bias
Where systemic scoring disparities are detected, a proportional correction is applied. Capped at ±15 points. Transparent in the governance record.
09
Explain
Three audience-appropriate explanations are generated: one for the candidate, one for the employer, one for the governance record. Every outcome has one.

Built-in fairness

Equal treatment,
not equal outcomes.

FHP detects and corrects for systematic scoring disparities across demographic groups. It doesn't enforce quotas — it enforces consistent treatment. A qualified candidate from any background receives the same score as an equally qualified candidate from any other.

Three metrics — Disparate Impact Ratio, Equal Opportunity Difference, and Score Distribution Skew — are computed nightly across every company and every job. Breaches trigger automatic escalation to the Fairness Oversight Board.

Protocol Council

Six-member governing body. Stewards the protocol, approves changes, maintains the canonical version. No single company can control it. All votes are public.

🔍

Fairness Oversight Board

Independent watchdog with veto power. Fairness researchers, civil rights advocates, disability advocates, labour representatives. Publishes quarterly audit reports.

🔧

Technical Working Group

Open membership. Maintains schemas, ontology, and the reference implementation. Conducts technical reviews of all appeals. Cannot modify fairness logic.

Terms of Service

Fair Hiring Protocol ("FHP") is an open standard published under the Apache 2.0 licence. Use of this platform is subject to the terms below.

1. Eligibility

You must be 18 years of age or older to register as a candidate. Companies must be legally incorporated entities.

2. Candidate data

Your profile data is used exclusively for matching. Your identity is never shared with employers without your explicit consent. You may download, correct, or delete your data at any time.

3. Matching decisions

All match outcomes are produced by the published FHP pipeline. Every decision includes a plain-language explanation. You have the right to appeal any outcome within 30 days.

4. Company obligations

Companies are bound by FHP response SLAs. Ghosting triggers automatic escalation. Salary ranges must be accurate. Discriminatory job requirements are prohibited.

5. Governing law

This platform operates under the FHP Protocol Council's governance framework. Protocol changes require a supermajority of the Council and are subject to a 30-day public comment period.

Privacy Policy

FHP collects only the data required to operate the matching service. We do not sell data, run advertising, or share your information with third parties outside the matching pipeline.

Data we collect

Candidates: email address (for authentication), skills, work history, preferences, and — only with explicit consent — demographic data for fairness monitoring. Your name is never required or stored.

Companies: company name, registration number, and contact email. Job briefs and match outcomes are stored for audit purposes.

Demographic data (special category)

Demographic data is processed under GDPR Article 9 explicit consent. It is write-only from the API — even FHP operators cannot read back individual values. It is used exclusively for the nightly fairness computation and is never shared with employers.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, export, and delete your data at any time from the Data & Privacy tab in your candidate portal. Deletion is permanent and processed within 30 days.

Retention

Match outcomes and appeal records are retained for 7 years for audit purposes. All other data is deleted when you close your account.
