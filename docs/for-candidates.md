# Fair Hiring Protocol — Guide for Candidates

The Fair Hiring Protocol (FHP) is designed to protect you, empower you, and ensure you are evaluated fairly.

This guide explains how FHP works from your perspective.

---

# 1. What FHP Guarantees You

- Transparent decisions  
- No arbitrary filtering  
- No discrimination  
- No ghosting  
- Control over your data  
- Clear explanations for matches and rejections  
- Ability to correct AI inferred skills  
- Ability to appeal unfair outcomes  

See `candidate-rights-charter.md` for full rights.

---

# 2. How Your Profile Works

Your profile is structured into:

- Skills (self declared + AI inferred)  
- Experience (roles, projects, non traditional work)  
- Preferences (work mode, salary, industries)  
- Constraints (availability, location, legal)  
- Optional demographics (for fairness monitoring only)  

You control:
- what is visible  
- what is hidden  
- what is corrected  
- what is deleted  

---

# 3. How Matching Works

The system evaluates:

1. Must have skills  
2. Nice to have skills  
3. Transferable skills  
4. Experience relevance  
5. Preference alignment  
6. Bias correction (if needed)  

Every decision is explained.

---

# 4. How to Improve Your Matches

- Confirm or reject inferred skills  
- Add project based experience  
- Add transferable skills  
- Clarify preferences  
- Update availability  

---

# 5. Appeals

If something feels wrong:
- request an explanation  
- request correction  
- request fairness review  

Your voice matters.

---

FHP exists to make hiring fair for you.  
You deserve clarity, dignity, and opportunity.
