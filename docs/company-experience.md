# Company Experience (CoX)

Companies get clarity, not power. 

They get accuracy, not control.

## 1. Job Brief Creation

Companies provide:
- 	required skills
- 	optional skills
- 	responsibilities
- 	constraints
- 	salary range (mandatory)
- 	working conditions
- 	seniority level

### 1.1. AI Assisted Cleanup

The system:
- 	removes fluff
- 	identifies contradictions
- 	separates must haves from nice to haves
- 	flags unrealistic expectations

Companies must confirm the cleaned brief.

## 2. Matching Experience

Companies receive:
- 	ranked candidates
- 	transparent explanations
- 	inferred skills
- 	constraint alignment
- 	bias aware adjustments

### 2.1. No Arbitrary Filters

Companies cannot filter by:
- 	age
- 	gender
- 	ethnicity
- 	university
- 	years since graduation
- 	employment gaps

They can filter by:
- 	skills
- 	constraints
- 	seniority
- 	availability
- 	location (if relevant)

### 2.2. Explanation First

Each candidate comes with:
- 	why they match
- 	what they bring
- 	what gaps exist
- 	how transferable skills compensate

This eliminates “gut feel” bias.

## 3. Accountability
Companies must:
- 	justify rejections
- 	follow fair process
- 	adhere to the protocol
- 	provide structured feedback

The system enforces:
- 	no ghosting
- 	no fishing expeditions
- 	no fake jobs
