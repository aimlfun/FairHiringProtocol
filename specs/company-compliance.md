# Company Compliance Requirements

Companies must meet these requirements to use the Fair Hiring Protocol.

---

# 1. Mandatory Job Brief Requirements

Companies must:

- Provide salary ranges  
- Provide real responsibilities  
- Separate must have from nice to have  
- Avoid arbitrary filters  
- Avoid demographic filtering  
- Avoid “years since graduation” filters  
- Avoid prestige-based filtering  

---

# 2. Behavioral Requirements

Companies must:

- Respond to all matched candidates  
- Provide structured rejection reasons  
- Maintain accurate job briefs  
- Avoid fishing expeditions  
- Avoid fake job postings  

---

# 3. Technical Requirements

Companies must:

- Accept match explanations  
- Log decisions  
- Allow fairness monitoring  
- Use the canonical protocol version  

---

# 4. Enforcement

Violations may result in:

- Warnings  
- Job suspension  
- Company suspension  
- Public fairness violation reports  

Compliance is mandatory for participation.
