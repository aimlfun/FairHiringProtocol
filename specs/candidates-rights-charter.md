# Candidate Rights Charter

The Fair Hiring Protocol exists primarily to protect candidates.  

This document defines the rights every candidate has when interacting with any FHP compliant system.

---

# 1. Transparency Rights

Candidates have the right to:

- Understand why they were matched  
- Understand why they were rejected  
- See how their score was computed  
- See whether bias correction was applied  
- Know which version of the protocol was used  

---

# 2. Control Rights

Candidates have the right to:

- Control profile visibility  
- Hide sensitive attributes  
- Correct AI inferred skills  
- Delete their profile  
- Export their data  
- Opt out of matching  

---

# 3. Fair Treatment Rights

Candidates have the right to:

- Be evaluated on skills, not pedigree  
- Be free from arbitrary filtering  
- Be free from discrimination  
- Receive structured feedback  
- Avoid ghosting  

---

# 4. Appeal Rights

Candidates may:

- Challenge incorrect inferences  
- Challenge unfair rejections  
- Request fairness review  
- Request audit logs  

---

# 5. Privacy Rights

Candidates have the right to:

- Minimal data retention  
- No surveillance  
- No behavioral profiling  
- No unauthorized data sharing  

---

*This charter is binding for all FHP compliant systems.*
