Your Data Rights
📥 Access & Export
Download everything FHP holds about you in open FHP JSON format — your profile, match history, explanations, and appeal records. Your right to receive a copy of your personal data and to take it elsewhere. Fulfilled instantly, any time.
✏️ Rectification
Correct inaccurate data at any time via the My Profile tab. Changes are immediate. Your right to have inaccurate personal data corrected without undue delay.
🗑️ Delete Account
Your email, profile, and login credentials are permanently deleted. Historical match records are retained in fully anonymised form — your identifier is replaced with a new random UUID with no link to you — to preserve the governance audit trail. This is permitted where retention serves a legitimate purpose and you cannot be re-identified from the retained data. This cannot be undone.

Fairness Monitoring Data not provided

FHP cannot police discrimination without knowing who is being discriminated against. Providing this data is entirely optional and has zero effect on your match scores. It is used only by the nightly fairness computation to detect whether certain groups are being treated differently. You can provide some fields, all, or none.

Explicit consent required before we collect this data. Sex, ethnicity, religion, and birth year are special category personal data under GDPR Article 9. This data is stored in a separate, maximally-restricted partition. The matching engine has no access to it — ever. Only the fairness computation service can read it, in aggregate only.

You can withdraw this consent at any time. Your data will be deleted and your cohort memberships removed. This will not affect your matches.

FHP was built around your rights. Not as a legal formality — as the actual reason it exists. Every technical decision in the protocol traces back to one of these thirteen commitments.

01
The right to a fair match
Your match score is computed by a deterministic, public formula applied consistently to every candidate. No arbitrary filters. No credential proxies. No black box. You can read the formula in the specification and verify it yourself.
Equality Act 2010 · EU Equal Treatment Directives · FHP Scoring Spec
02
The right to an explanation
Every match outcome comes with a plain-language explanation automatically — you never have to request it. It tells you which skills matched, which did not, what transferred, and what you could do differently. The explanation is generated at the moment of matching and is permanent.
UK GDPR Article 22 · EU GDPR Article 22 · EU AI Act Article 13
03
The right to human review
You may appeal any automated match outcome within 30 days. The Technical Working Group reviews the full pipeline trace. The Protocol Council makes the final decision. If an error is found, your outcome is corrected. This process is free, unconditional, and has defined time limits.
UK GDPR Article 22(3) · EU AI Act Article 14 · Equality Act 2010
04
The right against ghosting
Companies are contractually bound to respond within defined timeframes at every hiring stage. When they breach these commitments, FHP detects it automatically, notifies you, and escalates. Three breaches in 90 days pauses a company's hiring. You will always know where you stand.
FHP Company Compliance Agreement · Governance Escalation Spec
05
The right to anonymised matching
Employers see your skills profile and match score. They do not see your name, email address, or any identifying information unless you explicitly consent to share it. The matching engine itself operates on a random identifier and your skills data — it cannot see who you are by design.
UK GDPR Articles 5 and 25 · Privacy by Design (EU AI Act Article 9)
06
The right to access your data
Download a complete copy of everything FHP holds about you at any time, immediately, from your account. The export is in open FHP JSON schema, so you can read it, use it, and import it to any FHP-compliant system.
UK GDPR Article 15 · EU GDPR Article 15 · CCPA (California residents) · LGPD (Brazil)
07
The right to correct your data
Update any field of your profile at any time. Corrections are immediate and reflected in subsequent match runs. No approval process, no waiting period.
UK GDPR Article 16 · EU GDPR Article 16
08
The right to delete your account
When you delete your account, your email, login credentials, and skills profile are deleted. Historical match records are pseudonymised — your identifier is replaced with a new random UUID with no link to you. The anonymised records are retained for the governance audit trail to defend against discrimination claims. Your personal data is gone.
UK GDPR Article 17 · EU GDPR Article 17(3) · FHP Pseudonymisation Procedure
09
The right to data portability
Your profile is expressed in a publicly documented open schema. Export it and use it with any other FHP-compliant implementation. You are not locked in to any platform.
UK GDPR Article 20 · EU GDPR Article 20
10
The right to transparent bias monitoring
FHP monitors for systematic scoring disparities across demographic groups every night. Three metrics — Disparate Impact Ratio, Equal Opportunity Difference, and Score Distribution Skew — are publicly documented. When a metric breaches its threshold for three consecutive windows, a mandatory Fairness Oversight Board review is triggered.
Equality Act 2010 · EU Equal Treatment Directives · NYC Local Law 144 (2023) · EEOC AI Guidance (2023)
11
The right to be evaluated on capability, not credentials
Companies using FHP cannot require a specific degree, graduation year, institution, or years of experience as a hard filter. These are prohibited because they constitute indirect age and socioeconomic discrimination. Your skills are what matter — not where you learned them or how long ago. When you see the education field on your profile, it says the same thing: it has no effect on your matches.
Equality Act 2010 · EU Directive 2000/78/EC · FHP Company Compliance Agreement §3.3
12
The right to a structured rejection
If a company decides not to progress your application, they must send a structured rejection within their committed SLA, using a defined reason code and stage-specific notes. Vague rejections, or no response at all, are compliance violations enforced by FHP.
FHP Company Compliance Agreement §4.2 · Governance Escalation Spec
13
The right to participate for free
FHP is free for candidates. You will never be charged to create a profile, appear in matches, receive explanations, submit appeals, or exercise any right on this page. This is a foundational commitment of the protocol, written into the governance charter and requiring a Protocol Council vote to change.
FHP Candidate Rights Charter §13 · Governance Charter
Want to see how companies are performing? The FHP Governance Dashboard is public. It shows fairness metrics, active escalations, company compliance scores, and every Protocol Council decision. If a company has ghosting strikes or a fairness breach, it is there. You have the right to know.
