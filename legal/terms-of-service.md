## 2. What FHP does

FHP is an automated job matching service. It compares the skills and preferences you declare in your profile against job requirements posted by companies. It produces a match score and a plain-language explanation for every comparison made.

**FHP does not:**
- Guarantee you employment or interviews
- Guarantee that companies will respond to your profile
- Represent or act as an agent for any company
- Make hiring decisions — it makes matching recommendations, which companies act on

**FHP does:**
- Treat you as a skills profile, not a name or résumé
- Give you a full explanation of every match outcome
- Enforce companies' commitments to respond within defined timeframes
- Allow you to appeal any automated outcome
- Protect your identity from employers until you choose to share it

### 3.1 Registration

You create an account with your email address. We use your email only to authenticate you and send you notifications. Your email is never shared with employers.

You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure. Notify us immediately at [security@fair-hiring-protocol.org] if you suspect unauthorised access.

### 3.2 One account per person

You may hold one active account. Creating multiple accounts to circumvent a suspension or to game the matching system is a breach of these Terms and will result in permanent account closure.

### 3.3 Accurate information

The skills and proficiency levels you declare in your profile must be honest. Claiming proficiency you do not have misleads employers, corrupts the fairness metrics FHP relies on, and wastes everyone's time including yours. Persistent misrepresentation may result in account suspension.

## 4. The automated matching process

FHP uses automated processing to match your profile to jobs. This is explained in the Candidate Rights Charter and Privacy Policy. In summary:

- The matching formula is public — you can read exactly how your score is calculated
- Every outcome comes with a plain-language explanation
- You can appeal any outcome within 30 days
- Human governance bodies review appeals and can overturn automated outcomes
- Your identity is never shared with employers without your explicit consent

By using FHP, you acknowledge that automated matching decisions will be made about your profile and you accept the appeal process as the mechanism for human review.
